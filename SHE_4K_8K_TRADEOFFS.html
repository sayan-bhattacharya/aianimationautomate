The key quality tradeoff

An upscale gives you a 4K/8K file and cleaner delivery compatibility, but it cannot invent fine detail absent from the 1080p render. This film’s Python scenes, text plates, sprites, and source imagery are built around a 1920 × 1080 frame, so most of the larger pixel grid is interpolated.

AreaWhat 4K/8K improvesWhat remains limited by 1080p sources
Viewing / platformHigher-resolution playback option; can look cleaner on large displays and may reduce platform re-compression.Not the same as native 4K/8K capture or rendering.
Edges and gradientsLanczos resampling gives smoother diagonals and less obvious stair-stepping; light sharpening restores some perceived crispness.Fine texture, thin typography, and tiny sprite detail cannot be recovered once missing.
CompositionSame framing, timing, colors, effects, and detail as the 1080p master.No extra visual fidelity from larger output dimensions alone.
Storage / delivery4K and 8K compatibility and better crop/reframe room for some workflows.Much larger files, longer upload/processing, and 8K playback demands.

What is affected if you stay at 1080p?

You keep the film’s authored visual quality and avoid a second encode. You give up the larger delivery raster, some flexibility for cropping/reframing, and the chance that a platform’s 4K rendition looks better after its own compression. On a 4K screen, a good 1080p master is still watchable, but the display must scale it and fine edges can look softer.

For this asset set, 4K upscale is the practical delivery choice. 8K mainly makes sense when you specifically need 8K delivery, deep crop room, or want to compare platform encodes; it does not make the mushrooms, dinosaurs, water, or titles intrinsically more detailed.

Commands added

After the master exists, export without re-rendering the film:

python -m she_film.render_3min --upscale-only --4k
python -m she_film.render_3min --upscale-only --8k

The export uses Lanczos scaling, restrained unsharp masking, H.264 CRF 17, and copies the existing audio stream. Output: she_full_song_master_4k.mp4 or she_full_song_master_8k.mp4.

Estimate caveat: actual encode time varies with Colab CPU allocation, FFmpeg build, disk/Drive throughput, bitrate, and runtime throttling. The provided measured baseline is a planning anchor, not a benchmark of this exact checkout. Direct native 4K/8K estimates also depend on sources being regenerated at those resolutions; existing plates and caches are 1080p-oriented.
